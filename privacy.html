Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026. Applies to the 3D Zoo iPhone app and the 3D Zoo website.

The short version: 3D Zoo does not collect, store, or share any information about you. Your zoo is saved only on your own device.

What the game stores. Your saved zoo (money, animals, buildings, staff, and so on) and a few game settings, such as map scrolling speed, are kept on your device so you can pick up where you left off. In the iPhone app, a backup copy of your save is also kept in the app's own private storage on your phone. None of this is sent anywhere.

What the game does not do.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • No collection of personal information, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
  • The iPhone app makes no internet connections while you play.

Exporting a save. If you choose Menu → Export save file, the game creates a file of your zoo and opens the iPhone share sheet (or a download, in a browser). Where that file goes, such as Files, AirDrop, or a message, is entirely up to you.

Deleting your data. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes your saved zoo and its backup. In a browser, clearing the website's data for this site does the same. Menu → Start a new zoo replaces your current zoo.

The website. The browser version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, when the page loads; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself sends nothing to GitHub or anyone else.

Children. 3D Zoo is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Found a bug, have a question, or have an idea for a new animal? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page:

github.com/amangerian/3DZoo/issues

A free GitHub account is needed to post. Please include your 3D Zoo version number (shown next to the title at the top of the game) and, for the app, your iPhone model.

Common questions

  • How do I move around the map? With the Look tool selected, drag with one finger. Pinch to zoom. To make dragging move the map farther, change Menu → Map scrolling.
  • How do I move my zoo to another device? Use Menu → Export save file on the old device, then Menu → Import save file on the new one.
  • Where is my zoo saved? On your device only. See the privacy policy above.
